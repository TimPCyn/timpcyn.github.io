Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Who this app is for

Hermes Clanker is a personal automation tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

With the owner's consent, the app accesses the owner's own Google account data: Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Tasks, Google Drive, Google Contacts, Google Sheets, Google Docs, Google Forms and Apps Script.

How the data is used

The data is used only to perform tasks the owner requests, such as reading booking emails, creating calendar events and reminders, and managing documents.

Sharing

Data is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising. Data is stored only on the owner's private server and the AI model provider used to process the owner's requests.

Google API Services

Hermes Clanker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email address shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
